# Hi there! 👋

👨🏻‍💻 My name is Ian Guelman;

🚀 I'm co-founder & CTO at [Vom](https://vomdecision.com), where we're building an AI-powered decision intelligence platform that helps companies automate, measure, and continuously improve their business decisions — processing 10M+ decisions/month;

🎓 I hold an MSc in Computer Science from UFMG, where my dissertation focused on assessing the effectiveness of large language models in code comment generation, and a BSc in Software Engineering from PUC Minas;

📚 I'm active in academic research, with papers published in national and international conferences in Software Engineering and AI;

📍 I live in Belo Horizonte, Brazil.
